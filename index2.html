The LittleJS Arcade

A growing collection of tiny arcade games — all open source, all built with the LittleJS engine. They play instantly in your browser, no installs.

What is LittleJS?

A tiny, fast, zero-dependency HTML5 game engine. It powers every game in this arcade.
